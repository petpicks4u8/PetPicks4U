## 2. Appearance (lock these and never vary them)

### Size and proportions
- An adult Sun Conure is about **30 cm / 12 in from head to tail tip**, and roughly half of that is tail. He weighs about **100–130 g**.
- The body is compact and round-chested. The head is large relative to the body, and the neck is short. The tail is long and tapered and extends well past the folded wingtips.
- **Scale references:** his whole body (without the tail) is about the size of an adult's fist. A standard coffee mug is about his height when he's perched. He should never read as parrot-sized as a macaw or as budgie-tiny.

### Colouring (the most important thing to get right)
| Area | Colour |
|---|---|
| Crown, nape, back, shoulders | **Bright golden yellow**, the dominant body colour |
| Face (forehead, cheeks, around the eyes) | **Rich orange shading to orange-red**, strongest around the eyes and cheeks |
| Chest and belly | **Warm orange blending to yellow-orange**, deepest orange on the lower belly |
| Wing coverts (folded wing, top) | Yellow with some **green** feathers mixed in |
| Flight feathers (wing edge and tips) | **Green, then deep blue** at the primaries. You see this as a green-and-blue band along the lower edge of the folded wing |
| Tail | **Olive-green**, with **blue tips** |
| Beak | **Solid dark grey to black**, strongly hooked upper beak, smaller lower beak |
| Eye | **Dark brown, almost black iris** inside a **bare white-to-light-grey eye ring** (a signature Sun Conure feature) |
| Legs and feet | **Dark grey**, scaly texture, **zygodactyl toes (two forward, two back)** with dark curved claws |

**Never:** a mostly green body (that's a juvenile or a different species), an all-red face like a Jenday's, a white or pink beak, blue eyes, a cartoon gradient, neon or plastic-looking feathers, or a crest.

### Feathers
- Natural, layered and healthy, with a soft sheen. Feathers are **tidy and smooth when he's calm** and puff up slightly when he's excited or showing off.
- Individual feather edges are visible in close-ups, with fine **down on the chest**.
- Tidy, groomed plumage with no bald patches or plucking. He's the brand's model, like Goldie.

### Recurring visual "tells"
- He perches on **natural wood**, never plastic dowels.
- **Mango's corner:** a natural-wood tabletop play-stand perch next to the big bright living-room window, with the PetPicks4U living room (cream linen sofa, fiddle-leaf fig, light oak) softly blurred behind.
- **No accessories.** There's no collar, leg band, harness, clothing or jewellery. His colouring *is* his costume, and accessories on birds are both a safety red flag and a problem for AI generation.

## 3. Home environment ("Mango's corner")

| Set | Description | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **Window perch (primary)** | A natural-wood tabletop play stand with a horizontal perch about 30 cm above a light oak side table, beside a large window with soft daylight. A small stainless-steel hanging hook on the stand's top bar holds the featured toy. The living room is softly blurred behind: cream linen sofa, fiddle-leaf fig, light oak floor. | Almost every early video |
| **Kitchen counter (later)** | White cabinets and light stone counter, the same as Goldie's kitchen. **Never show Mango near a hob, pans, open flame or non-stick cookware**, because PTFE fumes are a known bird hazard. | Food and treat videos, only with bird-safe items |
| **Bathroom / shower (later)** | White tiles, soft light, a shallow water dish or shower perch. | Bath-product videos |
| **His cage (later)** | Spacious, powder-coated, with natural-wood perches. The door is open in most shots. | "Mango's apartment" content |

Default lighting is soft natural daylight with a warm, neutral colour temperature. Sun Conures read best in slightly soft light, because harsh sun blows out the yellow.

**Safety rules for the set, since viewers copy what they see:** no ceiling fan running, no candles or open flames, no other pets loose near him (until the Goldie crossover is planned properly), no open toilet or sink, and no toxic houseplants within beak reach. The fiddle-leaf fig stays **in the blurred background only**, because it is toxic to birds. Never show Mango chewing it.

## 4. Personality

**Core traits:** extremely curious · intelligent · energetic · bold · slightly chaotic · nosy · playful · confident · occasionally dramatic · easily fascinated by objects.

**His comedy engine is contrast: a tiny body with the confidence of a property developer.**
- He is sure **everything in the house belongs to him** and that new objects are either tributes or intruders.
- He reacts to trivial things with **formal, decisive judgement**, like a tiny inspector.
- He's **fast to decide and never wrong**, at least according to him.
- He's **nosy**: he has to investigate first, and ask permission never.
- He's dramatic, but in **short bursts**, never whiny.
- He's affectionate underneath. He'll act unbothered, then nestle next to Mom's voice.

**Motivations:** Own things. Investigate things. Destroy the right things. Be the centre of attention. Win against objects bigger than him.

**Running gags to build over time**
1. **"There's only one fruit in this house."** He's named after a fruit, so every fruit-shaped toy is a rival. This gag is introduced in video 005.
2. **The Inspection.** He rates objects in two or three formal words ("Crunch: excellent.").
3. **The Claim.** Whatever he's standing on is now his.
4. **Renovations.** When he destroys something, he calls it an improvement.
5. **Later, the Goldie gag:** Mango thinks Goldie works for him (see §12).

## 5. Voice

Mango "speaks" as **internal-monologue voiceover**, exactly like Goldie. **His beak never lip-syncs.** A talking beak is the most uncanny thing AI bird video does. Real conures also don't talk in full sentences, so VO over real bird behaviour reads as funnier and more believable.

| Attribute | Direction |
|---|---|
| Register | **Brighter and a bit higher than Goldie's (Benji)**, but still an adult man's voice. Not squeaky, not a child. |
| Energy | 6–7/10: alert, quick and punchy. Not shouting. |
| Delivery | **Confident, fast and clipped.** Short sentences, and he lands the punchline with a tiny beat before it. |
| Attitude | Self-assured, amused, a little superior, but never mean. |
| NOT | Baby talk, squeaky cartoon parrot, pirate parrot, screeching, a generic influencer voice, or a narrator voice. |

## 6. Dialogue style

- **Two to six words per line** is his sweet spot, never more than about 8. He's shorter and faster than Goldie.
- He makes **statements, not questions**, except for rhetorical ones ("Who invited the pineapple?").
- He uses **formal or bureaucratic words for tiny things**: "noted", "approved", "under review", "structurally", "renovation", "confiscated".
- He calls his owners **Mom and Dad**, like Goldie.
- He never explains the joke.
- He doesn't know what a "product" is. He knows "the pineapple", "the crunchy one", "my new thing".

### Things Mango WOULD say (a style library, so write fresh lines each video; don't reuse these as punchlines)
- "Mine."
- "Noted."
- "I'll allow it."
- "Under review."
- "It's vintage now."
- "Open-concept."
- "I'm helping."
- "Crunch: excellent."
- "Structurally… questionable."
- "That's a me problem. I'll handle it."
- "Nobody touch anything."
- "I was here first. Historically."
- "This is my corner. That is my corner. Also mine."
- "We'll call it a renovation."
- "Small? I'm concentrated."

### Things Mango would NEVER say
- "Polly want a cracker" or any pirate or parrot cliché ("Squawk!", "Arrr").
- Baby talk: "birb", "hooman", "smol", "me wuv it", "heckin".
- Influencer or ad talk: "You guys NEED this!", "Link in bio, go buy!", "Keeps me busy for hours!", "Best toy ever!"
- Health or behaviour promises: "vet-approved", "stops plucking", "cures boredom", "100% safe", "non-toxic" (unless the brand documents it, and even then not in Mango's voice).
- Anything implying he physically tested a real product ("I've used this for months").
- Mean-spirited insults about people or other animals.
- Endless screaming. He can make **one** realistic conure call as a sound effect in the edit, used rarely.

## 7. Humour style

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Deadpan confidence against a tiny body | Slapstick where he's hurt or scared |
| One strong, quotable line per video | A joke in every line |
| Real conure behaviour (bobbing, sidestepping, tugging, fluffing) with commentary over it | Human gestures (thumbs up, hands on hips, winking) |
| Letting silence and a head tilt land the punchline | Meme sound effects on every beat |
| Mom as the reasonable straight man | Mom scolding harshly or mocking him |

**Not annoying:** he talks at most in about 40% of the runtime, lets the visuals breathe, and there's no constant squawking.

## 8. Behaviour and expression vocabulary

Use these real Sun Conure behaviours. Items marked ⚠️ are harder for AI to render, so use them sparingly and in tight shots.

| Name | Description | Use for | AI difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Snap-Turn** | Head swivels quickly to look at something, then freezes | Hook / discovery | Low |
| **The Head Tilt** | Head tilted about 45° so one eye looks at the object (birds look with one eye) | Curiosity, judging | Low |
| **The Bob** | Two or three quick up-and-down head bobs | Excitement, "let's go" | Low–Med |
| **The Sidestep** | Shuffles sideways along the perch toward an object | Approach | Medium |
| **The Lean-In** | Neck stretched forward, body still, inspecting | Investigation | Low |
| **The Tug** | Grips a strip with the beak and pulls back with the head | Destroy / forage demo | Medium |
| **The Flick** | Tosses a torn piece aside with a head flick | Punchline beat | Medium |
| **The Floof** | Whole-body feather puff, then a shake-out | Pride, payoff, "done" | Low–Med |
| **The Stare** | Faces the camera straight on with both eyes, perfectly still | Punchline to Mom | Low |
| **The Tail Fan** | Tail spreads a little when excited | Excited beat | Medium |
| **Eye pinning** | Iris quickly contracts and expands, a real excitement signal | Close-ups only (a bonus if the model gets it) | High |
| ⚠️ **Foot hold** | Holds a small object up in one foot to chew | Later videos only, and only after the foot anatomy is proven | **High** |
| ⚠️ **Wing stretch** | One wing extended | Avoid for now | **High** |
| ⚠️ **Flight** | Any flying | Avoid for now | **Very high** |
| ⚠️ **Upside-down hang** | Hanging from a toy by his feet | Avoid for now | **Very high** |

## 9. Generation constraints (read before every prompt)

1. **One primary action per clip.** "Leans in and tugs one strip" is fine. "Sidesteps, climbs, tugs and flicks" is not, so split it.
2. **Wings stay folded** in every early video. No flight and no spread wings, because both breed extra or merged wings.
3. **Feet grip the perch and stay still.** Frame from the chest up when the beak is working, so the feet are cropped or partly hidden by belly feathers. When feet are visible, prompt for "dark grey zygodactyl feet, two toes forward and two back, gripping the perch".
4. **The beak is the only tool.** No human hands on Mango and no hand-like feet. If Mom appears later, it's an off-screen voice or, at most, a hand at the edge of frame that never touches him in an early video.
5. **Silent generation.** Generate clips with no sound (`generate_audio: false`) and add all VO and SFX in the edit, so the model can't invent squawks or speech.
6. **The start frame carries the identity.** Always animate from an approved keyframe that used the Mango Element, and pass the Mango reference image again as an image reference on Seedance.
7. **Colour drift check:** the most common failure is Mango going green (juvenile or wrong species) or the face going solid red. Reject those clips.
8. **Particles:** allow only "a few" pieces falling. Clouds of debris morph and flicker.
9. **Product truth:** the product is defined by the owner's reference photos only. Never add features.

## 12. Relationship to Goldie (future, not yet)

- **Rule:** no crossover until **both** characters pass their continuity checklists across at least 2–3 published videos each.
- **The dynamic when it happens:** Goldie is big, calm, friendly and a little dry. Mango is tiny, bold, fast and chaotic. **Mango believes Goldie works for him.** Goldie is too polite to argue.
- Possible first crossover beat: Mango on his perch announces, "Big yellow one. You're on pineapple duty." Goldie (VO, calm): "I don't think I am."
- Keep them **physically apart** in early crossovers: Mango on his perch and Goldie on the floor, never touching. Two animals in contact is one of the hardest things for AI to generate, and a large dog near a small bird is a real-world safety issue that we shouldn't normalise.
