## Customer problem
A bored dog with a strong nose and nothing to do makes his own fun: toilet paper, cushions, shoes.

## Main selling point
It gives a dog's nose a job. He sniffs out and forages for his food or treats instead of wolfing them down.

## Honesty guardrails (important for this product)
- ✅ OK to say: "gives a bored nose a job", "enrichment", "makes mealtime a sniffing game", "slows down fast eaters".
- ❌ Don't say or imply: "stops destructive chewing", "cures anxiety", "fixes behaviour", "keeps him busy for hours", "indestructible". Destruction can come from separation anxiety or other issues a mat won't fix.
- The video can show Goldie *choosing* the mat over the couch as comedy. The captions must frame it as boredom and enrichment, not a behaviour cure.

## Safety line (pinned comment and replies)
"Supervise sniff time: fleece isn't a chew toy. If your dog shreds things when left alone, that can be anxiety, so a trainer or vet is the right call."
