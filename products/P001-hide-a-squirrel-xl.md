## Main customer problem
Bored, under-stimulated dogs (especially retrievers/hunting breeds) who need a "job."

## Main selling point
A hide-and-seek puzzle a dog understands instantly: squirrels go in the tree, dog pulls squirrels out. Reloadable by the owner in seconds.

## Why it fits Goldie
- Goldie's running nemesis is a squirrel named Kevin → this toy *is* six Kevins.
- Retriever behaviour (pulling, carrying, guarding) is exactly what the toy invites.

## Why it fits short-form
- Instantly readable: a tree with squirrel heads poking out.

## Drawbacks / honesty notes
- Plush; rated for light chewers. Some reviewers' dogs shred squirrels fast.
- Not every squirrel may squeak (per some reviews).
- We will **not** claim durability, calming effects, or "hours" of play.

## Safety line (use in pinned comment / replies)
