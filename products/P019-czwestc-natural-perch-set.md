## Fit for Mango (Sun Conure)

- **Branch thickness:** conure guidance runs from about ½ in to 1 in. The set's 0.6–1.0 in mix sits right in that range ✅
- **Length:** each piece is short, about 6.5 in, so these are **extra spots around the cage**, not the main perch. Petco suggests a main perch of at least 9 in for a sun conure.
- **Listing says "for macaws":** misleading. These pieces are far too small for a macaw. We say nothing about macaws.

## Things to know (honest limitations)

- The pieces are short, so add them alongside a longer main perch rather than instead of one.
- Natural wood gets chewed (good for birds) and has to be replaced as it wears.
- The hardware is metal of unknown grade, but it sits mostly outside the bars. Check it now and then, and replace any piece that's chewed down to the bolt.
- Bark varies piece to piece; that's normal for natural wood.
- Clean perches when they get soiled; wet wood should dry fully before reuse.
