# New Product Playbook

## What the owner sends (pick one)

**A. Category research (recommended)**
> "Let's do video #2. Research the best dog water fountain for Goldie. Budget under $40."

**B. A specific product**
> "Let's do video #2 with this product: [Amazon link or exact name]."

**C. A problem to solve**
> "Next video: dogs who drink too fast / get bored in the car / hate bath time. Find a product."

Optional extras, in any order: an idea for the story, the tone you want, must-haves ("big-dog size", "under $30"), and reference photos of the product (upload them to Higgsfield the same way as for the snuffle mat).

## What Claude does (stops at ⏸ for approval)

| Step | Output | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Research | 3–6 candidates, scorecard, 1 pick + 2 backups → `research/`, `product-database.csv` | free |
| ⏸ | Owner confirms product, checks live price/rating on Amazon, uploads 3–6 product photos to Higgsfield | — |
| 2. Concepts | 3–5 story ideas using Goldie's personality, 1 recommended | free |
| ⏸ | Owner picks / tweaks the story | — |
| 3. Script + prompts + captions | `scripts/`, `higgsfield-prompts/`, `captions/` | free |
| 4. Scene stills | ~9 keyframes with the Goldie element + product element | ~20 credits |
| ⏸ | Owner approves stills (or names scenes to redo) | — |
| 5. Clips + voice + rough cut | video clips, Benji voice lines, assembled captioned + clean cuts | ~120–150 credits |
| ⏸ | Owner reviews; tweaks cost ~10–25 credits per clip | — |
| 6. Owner finishes | music/SFX in CapCut, affiliate link in bio, post with AI + #ad labels | — |
| 7. After 48 h / 7 days | Owner shares stats → Claude fills `analytics/performance-tracker.csv` and suggests the next video | free |

Typical total per video: **~150–200 Higgsfield credits**.
