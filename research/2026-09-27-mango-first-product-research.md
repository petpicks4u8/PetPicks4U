## 1. Bird-product safety screen (applied to every candidate)

Bird products need more scrutiny than dog products. Birds are small, they explore with their beaks, and they're very sensitive to toxins. These are the red flags I screened for, from avian toy-safety guidance (sources at the bottom):

| Hazard | Why it matters | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Zinc / lead hardware** (cheap bells, chains, wire, some quick-links) | Heavy-metal poisoning; small amounts can be fatal | Prefer no metal, or **stainless steel (304/316)** |
| **Bells with removable clappers** | Clapper swallowed; toe or beak caught | Avoid, or welded clapper only |
| **Cotton rope / long thin fibres** | Crop impaction; strands wrap around toes or neck | Prefer short-breaking natural fibres (sisal, abaca, seagrass, palm) and trim frays |
| **Loops, chains, rings of the wrong size** | Head, foot or wing entanglement | Loops too small for a head to pass through, or too big to trap one; hang toys short |
| **Unknown dyes / coatings / paint** | Ingestion toxicity | Food-grade or vegetable dyes only; **flag when not documented** |
| **Small detachable parts** | Choking | Parts must be too big to swallow for the bird's size |
| **Sharp edges / splinters** (as a toy is destroyed) | Cuts, crop injury | Remove the toy when its inner frame is exposed |
| **Wrong size** | Too small = swallowing and entanglement; too big = unused or fear | Match the **conure / medium-small parrot** size |
| **Water** | Parrots can't swim; slips; chilling | ≤ about 1 in (2.5 cm) deep, lukewarm, **always supervised** |
| **Electrical** (pumps, cords, USB) | Chewed cords mean electrocution | Reject unless the cord is fully out of reach |
| **Fabric huts / tents** | Fibre ingestion, entanglement; also linked to hormonal behaviour | Rejected category for Mango |
| **Mirrors** | Can encourage fixation or hormonal behaviour in some parrots | Not for Mango's first videos |
| **Treats** | Some human foods are toxic (avocado, chocolate, caffeine, alcohol, salty or sugary snacks) | Show only bird-safe items, or none |

**Standing rule:** an Amazon listing saying "safe" or "non-toxic" is **not** proof. Where I couldn't establish safety, it's flagged below.

## 4. ✅ Decision: Planet Pleasures Pineapple Foraging Toy, **Medium** (B00GV6ZWXC)

**Listing:** https://www.amazon.com/Planet-Pleasures-Pineapple-Foraging-Medium/dp/B00GV6ZWXC
**Brand:** Planet Pleasures, a natural-material toy maker whose products are made in the Philippines (brand, SNIPPET).
**What it is:** a pineapple-shaped foraging and shredding toy. Colourful woven **palm leaf** on the outside and part of the stuffing, **abaca fibre** for the loop and part of the stuffing, and **bamboo strips** forming the pineapple-shaped frame. Treats can be tucked between the weave (brand description, SNIPPET). It hangs from a **fabric (abaca) loop** at the top.
**Size:** Medium is about **7 × 2.5 × 2.5 in**, 1.06 oz (Amazon, SNIPPET). The Small is listed at 5 × 2 × 2 in on amazon.ca (SNIPPET). **Colours vary** from unit to unit (Chewy title, SNIPPET).
**Price:** US price **UNVERIFIED**. It's a low-cost impulse item (Small about CA$12 on amazon.ca, SNIPPET). **Please check.**
**Rating / reviews:** 4.6★ / 2,359 (Small listing, SNIPPET; Amazon often pools ratings across sizes) · Large 4.6★ / 584 (SNIPPET) · Chewy Small 4.5★ / 519 (SNIPPET).
**Evidence of demand:** a long-running multi-size listing (ASIN family since about 2013), a 2-pack variant, sold by Chewy, Walmart and specialist bird stores. Medium ranks about **#408 in Bird Toys** (SNIPPET). Reviewers report re-buying ("my Sun Conure's favourite toy… had to buy a second or third one"; "on his second one", SNIPPET).

### What problem it addresses
Parrots are driven to **chew, shred and forage**, and bored parrots find other things to destroy. This gives a conure something **meant** to be destroyed. We frame it only as **enrichment and fun**. We never promise to stop plucking, screaming or any behaviour problem.

### Why Mango can demonstrate it well
- **Shredding is the real behaviour.** Sun Conure owners literally describe their birds "pulling every little string out of it until it is completely annihilated" (review, SNIPPET). Mango doing that is honest to the product.
- The action is **one beak, one strip, one tug**, which is simple enough to generate shot by shot.
- **The comedy is built in:** Mango is named after a fruit and this toy is a fruit. The "rival fruit" joke is instantly understood with no setup.

### Why it could stop the scroll
In the first second there's a vivid yellow-orange bird nose-to-nose with a colourful pineapple the same size as him, plus the line *"Who invited the pineapple?"* It's absurd and readable without sound. Colour contrast also matters: saturated yellow and orange with a multicoloured pineapple pops in a feed.

### Safety considerations (honest)
| Check | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Materials | Palm leaf, abaca and bamboo, all natural plant fibres (brand, SNIPPET) | ✅ |
| Metal / bells | **None.** It hangs from a fabric loop | ✅ |
| Fibre type | Palm and abaca are the short-breaking natural fibres avian guidance prefers over cotton | ✅ with monitoring |
| **Dyes** | The palm is **coloured**, and **I couldn't confirm what dye is used** (vegetable or food-grade isn't stated in anything I could reach) | ⚠️ **UNVERIFIED**. Never call it "non-toxic" or "dye-safe" in captions |
| **Hanging loop** | Abaca loop: keep it **short**. Owners typically hang such toys with a **stainless-steel quick-link** (not zinc-plated). A long, loose loop is an entanglement risk | ⚠️ Scene shows a short hang from a **stainless hook** |
| **Ingestion** | Shredders should shred, not eat. Some birds swallow fibres | ⚠️ Supervision; remove if the bird eats the material |
| **Bamboo frame** | As it's destroyed, the bamboo strips are exposed, and they can splinter | ⚠️ Replace when the frame is exposed |
| **Size** | Small is sized for budgie to green-cheek; **Medium for a Sun Conure** | ✅ Link Medium |
| **Treats** | Optional. Only bird-safe treats | ✅ The video shows **no treats** (keeps the AI simple and avoids food-safety issues) |

**Our video rules for this product:** Mango shreds only the outer palm weave; no long strands wrap around him; he never visibly swallows material; the toy hangs short from a stainless hook; there's no treat in shot; and Mom's voice is present, implying supervision. The caption and pinned comment say **"supervise; replace when worn."**

### AI-generation difficulty: **Medium (manageable)**
- ✅ Soft woven material deforms plausibly, so small mistakes read as "palm leaf bending".
- ✅ Feet stay gripping the perch the whole video, and wings stay folded.
- ⚠️ The **beak gripping a strip and tugging** is the hard moment. It's split into its own 5-second clip with a fallback (start with the strip already in his beak).
- ⚠️ **Colours vary** on the real product, so the owner's reference photos lock one colourway and we tell the model to match them.
- ⚠️ Keep falling debris to "a few strips", never a cloud.

### Affiliate / content potential
A cheap, **consumable** product that owners re-buy is ideal for affiliate volume. It launches Mango's "**rival fruit**" franchise, which carries on to other fruit-shaped toys, the same "Kevin" logic that works for Goldie.

**Content multiplication (at least 8 original videos):**
1. *Who Invited the Pineapple?* Mango discovers the rival (first video).
2. *Renovation.* It opens on the wreckage: "Open-concept."
3. *The Inspection.* Mango rates it in three words.
4. *Mom Tries to Take It Away.* "It's evidence."
5. *Mango POV.* A tight shred close-up with running commentary.
6. *Silent ASMR.* Crunch, shred, no VO, "sound on 🔊".
7. *The Replacement.* A new pineapple arrives: "They sent another one."
8. *Size Challenge.* The Large pineapple is bigger than Mango: "I've fought bigger." (Only if we're happy to show the Large with a conure, since the Medium is the recommended size.)
9. *Later: Goldie meets the pineapple.* Mango: "Don't. It's mine." (Crossover rules apply.)

**Confidence:** moderate-high, about 70%. Fit, safety profile and AI feasibility are strong. The weak spots are the **unconfirmed dye** and the **unverified US price and rating**, which you need to check.
