## Creative strategy

- **Customer problem:** most cages ship with smooth identical dowels. Owners don't know that's an issue until someone tells them.
- **Angle:** Mango is *bored* of standing on the same stick. His feet "have notes." Then real branches show up and he tests each one like a connoisseur.
- **Why it feels natural:** it's entirely about feet. Mango's early-video canon is wings folded, feet on the perch, so this product plays straight to what we can generate reliably.
- **The issue, told honestly:** one short on-screen fact sourced from avian care guidance: identical perches mean the same pressure points all day, and vets recommend mixing sizes and textures. We never say the product prevents or treats anything.
