## Why this hook works

- **Frame 1 is an emotional mismatch:** a bright yellow bird in a grey, rainy, sad-movie scene. That stops the scroll.
- **The caption calls out the viewer directly:** "If your bird's perch looks like this". Every owner with a stock dowel checks their own cage.
- **Real stakes in the first 10 s**, all sourced: same pressure points → sore feet.
- **Grey → colour** gives a satisfying turn that makes the product the hero without a sales pitch.
