# Video #001: Five Concepts for the Hide-A-Squirrel XL

Product: P001 Outward Hound Hide-A-Squirrel XL · Character: Goldie · Running gag introduced here: **Kevin**, the squirrel Goldie is at war with.

---

## Concept A: "The Stakeout" (Goldie discovers it)
- **Hook (0–2s):** Extreme close-up of Goldie's eye, pupil widening. Hard cut to a squirrel head poking out of a plush tree in the living room.
- **Setup:** Goldie creeps toward the tree in slow motion, belly low.
- **Conflict/curiosity:** Every time she gets close, she notices *another* squirrel head. She freezes. Head tilt.
- **Demo:** She noses into a hole, yanks a squirrel out, and it squeaks. Then a second.
- **Payoff:** A wide shot shows six squirrels scattered across the rug and Goldie lying in the middle, exhausted and proud. VO: "The neighbourhood is safe."
- **CTA:** "The tree's in my bio. The squirrels are not my problem anymore."

## Concept B: "Goldie's Honest Review" (POV/review)
- **Hook:** Goldie sits upright facing the camera like a critic, the tree beside her. VO: "I've been asked to review a tree."
- **Setup:** "It's a tree. Full of squirrels."
- **Conflict:** "Six. I counted. Twice." (She glances at the tree suspiciously.)
- **Demo:** Quick cuts of her pulling squirrels out, rated with on-screen paw icons.
- **Payoff:** "Four out of five bones. Minus one because they keep putting them back."
- **CTA:** "Review tree in bio." · *Risk:* reads more like an ad because the structure is a review.

## Concept C: "Boredom Crimes" (problem → product → reaction)
- **Hook:** A torn sofa cushion with stuffing everywhere. Goldie sits next to it and looks at the camera. VO: "We don't talk about the sofa."
- **Setup:** A human hand places the plush tree on the rug. Goldie is sceptical.
- **Conflict:** She ignores it, until a squirrel head is visible.
- **Demo:** She pulls squirrels out one by one.
- **Payoff:** A quiet living room, the sofa intact, Goldie asleep on a pile of squirrels. VO: "…The sofa and I have an understanding now."
- **CTA:** "Sofa-saving tree in bio." · *Risk:* a destruction image is harder to render and edges toward an implied "solves chewing" claim.

## Concept D: "Eviction Notice" (comedy)
- **Hook:** Goldie stands over the tree like a landlord. VO: "Rent was due Tuesday."
- **Setup:** She pulls a squirrel out and drops it outside the rug. "Out."
- **Conflict:** She pulls another. "You too." Then another. The pile grows.
- **Demo:** Her pull-and-drop routine is the demo.
- **Payoff:** A human hand refills the tree. Goldie watches, then side-eyes the camera. "…They came back."
- **CTA:** "Their tree is in my bio. I'm not responsible for them."

## Concept E: "One of You Is Kevin" (curiosity)
- **Hook (0s):** An odd, instantly readable image: six plush squirrels lined up in a neat row on the rug, and Goldie lying behind them like a detective, chin low, eyes moving along the row. VO: "One of you is Kevin."
- **Setup:** A "2 hours earlier" flashback shows Goldie discovering the plush tree full of squirrel heads.
- **Conflict/curiosity:** She pulls them out one at a time as "suspects" (squeak, squeak).
- **Demo:** The pulling and lining up *is* the product demo, and you see the whole mechanic.
- **Payoff:** Back at the lineup, the human says off-screen, "Goldie, they're toys." Goldie side-eyes the camera: "That's what Kevin wants you to think." She then guards one squirrel between her paws. "It's always this one."
- **CTA:** "Kevin's tree is in my bio. Kevin is not invited."

**Selected: Concept E, "One of You Is Kevin."**
It opens on a visual question ("why are those squirrels lined up?"), gives Goldie a clear dry-detective personality straight away, shows the whole product mechanic without explaining it, and introduces a recurring character (Kevin) we can bring back in future videos, which is the "universe" hook.
