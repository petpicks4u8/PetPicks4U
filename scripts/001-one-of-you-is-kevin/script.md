# Video #001: "One of You Is Kevin" (production script and shot list)

| | |
|---|---|
| Product | P001 Outward Hound Hide-A-Squirrel XL (B005VS9WO6) |
| Concept | E, curiosity / detective comedy |
| Length | ~26 s |
| Format | 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920 |
| Platforms | TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| Voices | Goldie (internal-monologue VO), plus one off-screen human line |
| Set | Goldie's living room (light oak floor, round jute rug, cream sofa, fiddle-leaf fig, window light from the left) |
| Status | Ready to generate |

**Core rule:** Goldie's mouth never lip-syncs. Everything she "says" is her internal voice, laid over natural dog behaviour.

---

## SCENE 1: The Lineup (HOOK)
- **Duration:** 0:00–0:03.0 (3.0 s)
- **Visual:** Six plush squirrels sit in a neat, evenly spaced row across the jute rug, all facing the camera. Behind them, Goldie lies flat in a sphinx pose, chin low, only her eyes moving slowly from one squirrel to the next like a detective at a police lineup. The plush tree trunk sits blurred in the background.
- **Camera:** Low, floor-level, eye-level with the squirrels. Locked-off with a very slow push-in (about 5%). 35mm feel, shallow depth of field, squirrels sharp in the foreground, Goldie sharp behind them.
- **Goldie action:** Completely still except her eyes and one ear flick. Serious.
- **Product action:** The squirrels are static and lined up. The trunk is visible but out of focus.
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO, low, slow): *"One of you is Kevin."*
- **On-screen text:** Auto-caption of the VO only.
- **Sound:** Room tone. A soft single low "suspense" synth note or bass hit under the VO. No music yet.
- **Transition:** Hard cut plus a quick 2-frame white flash, and a "rewind" whoosh SFX.

## SCENE 2: Two Hours Earlier (SETUP)
- **Duration:** 0:03.0–0:06.5 (3.5 s)
- **Visual:** The plush tree trunk sits alone on the rug with 3–4 squirrel heads poking out of its holes. Goldie walks into frame from the right, stops next to it, and does a slow head tilt.
- **Camera:** Medium-wide at dog eye-level, static.
- **Goldie action:** Walks in at a relaxed pace, stops, tilts her head about 30°, ears perk.
- **Product action:** Squirrel heads sticking out of the trunk holes. Static.
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO): *"They thought I wouldn't notice the tree."*
- **On-screen text:** Top-left, small, typewriter style: **"2 hours earlier"** (0:03.0–0:04.5).
- **Sound:** Light, sneaky pizzicato music starts (low volume) and runs to 0:18.
- **Transition:** Cut on action (the head tilt finishes, then cut in close).

## SCENE 3: The Sniff (CURIOSITY)
- **Duration:** 0:06.5–0:08.5 (2.0 s)
- **Visual:** Close-up. Goldie's black nose pushes into one hole of the plush trunk and sniffs. A squirrel tail sticks out of a neighbouring hole.
- **Camera:** Close-up, side angle, static, shallow focus on her nose.
- **Goldie action:** Nose pushes gently into the hole, nostrils flare, whiskers move.
- **Product action:** The plush fabric dents softly under her nose.
- **Dialogue:** None.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Sound:** Exaggerated sniff-sniff SFX.
- **Transition:** Hard cut.

## SCENE 4: Suspect One (DEMONSTRATION)
- **Duration:** 0:08.5–0:11.0 (2.5 s)
- **Visual:** Goldie pulls her head back out of the trunk holding a plush squirrel in her mouth, then lifts her head, pleased.
- **Camera:** Medium close-up, front 3/4, static.
- **Goldie action:** Gentle pull backwards, head up, soft mouth (a retriever carry). Tail wag visible at the edge of frame.
- **Product action:** The squirrel slides out of the hole. The trunk wobbles slightly and settles.
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO), matter-of-fact: *"Suspect one."*
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Sound:** SQUEAK SFX timed to the pull (the key audio moment), then music continues.
- **Transition:** Jump cut.

## SCENE 5: Processing Suspects (ESCALATION)
- **Duration:** 0:11.0–0:13.5 (2.5 s)
- **Visual:** Goldie trots proudly toward the camera across the living room with a squirrel in her mouth, head high. Behind her, the trunk sits with fewer squirrel heads left.
- **Camera:** Low front angle, static; she walks toward the lens and fills more of the frame.
- **Goldie action:** Victory trot: head high, tail wagging, bouncy gait.
- **Product action:** One squirrel carried in her mouth.
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO), quicker: *"Two. …Three. Four."* (Speed-ramp the edit so it feels like a montage.)
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Sound:** Three quick squeaks under the counting.
- **Transition:** Match cut back to the Scene 1 composition.

## SCENE 6: The Interrogation (CONFLICT / PUNCHLINE)
- **Duration:** 0:13.5–0:18.0 (4.5 s)
- **Visual:** Back to the lineup, the same composition as Scene 1. Goldie in sphinx pose behind the row. On the human's line, her eyes slide slowly toward the camera: the Side-Eye.
- **Camera:** Same as Scene 1, static.
- **Goldie action:** Holds still, then a slow side-eye to the lens, holds it.
- **Product action:** Squirrels lined up (static).
- **Dialogue:**
  - HUMAN (off-screen, casual, amused): *"Goldie… they're toys."*
  - (0.7 s beat, music drops out)
  - GOLDIE (VO, flat): *"That's what Kevin wants you to think."*
- **On-screen text:** Captions only.
- **Sound:** Music cuts out on the side-eye (the silence is the joke). Optional tiny record-scratch, but *prefer silence*.
- **Transition:** Cut.

## SCENE 7: It's Always This One (PAYOFF)
- **Duration:** 0:18.0–0:21.5 (3.5 s)
- **Visual:** Goldie lies on the rug with ONE plush squirrel pinned gently between her front paws, looking straight into the lens. The rest of the lineup is blurred in the background.
- **Camera:** Close-medium, front, dog eye-level, very slow push-in.
- **Goldie action:** The Guard: chin slightly lowered, direct eye contact, one slow blink.
- **Product action:** One squirrel under her paws.
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO): *"It's always this one."*
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Sound:** Soft music resumes (the sneaky theme, resolved).
- **Transition:** Slow 6-frame cross-dissolve.

## SCENE 8: Kevin Is Not Invited (CTA)
- **Duration:** 0:21.5–0:26.0 (4.5 s)
- **Visual:** Wide cozy shot. Goldie lies on the rug next to the now-empty plush tree trunk, with squirrels scattered around her. She rests her chin on one squirrel and lets out a contented sigh.
- **Camera:** Wide, slightly elevated (about 1 m), static.
- **Goldie action:** The Flop into a chin-rest, slow tail thump.
- **Product action:** The trunk is fully visible and clearly readable, with squirrels around it (a clean "this is the product" frame).
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO): *"Kevin's tree is in my bio. …Kevin is not invited."*
- **On-screen text:**
  - Lower third (0:22–end): **"Goldie's squirrel tree 🌳 link in bio"**
  - Small, bottom corner: **"#ad · Amazon affiliate link · AI-generated video"**
- **Sound:** Music resolves with a small button ending, plus a single final squeak on the last frame (the loop trigger).
- **Transition:** Hard cut to black for 0 frames. The loop restarts on Scene 1.
