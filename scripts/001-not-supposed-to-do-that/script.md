# Video #001: "I'm Not Supposed to Do That" (production script and shot list)

| | |
|---|---|
| Product | P003 AWOOF Snuffle Mat, large (see the product card for which listing to use) |
| Idea | Owner's brief: Goldie is tempted by toilet paper and furniture, knows he shouldn't, and the snuffle mat gives his nose a better job |
| Length | ~28 s |
| Format | 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920 |
| Voices | Goldie (inner-monologue voiceover, male) and one off-screen human |
| Sets | Bathroom doorway (the toilet-paper roll), living room (sofa, jute rug), hallway |
| Running gags it starts | Goldie's "temptations", and "Ugh. I'm not supposed to do that." |

**Core rule:** Goldie never lip-syncs. His lines are his inner voice, laid over real dog behaviour. We never show actual destruction happening, only the *moment before* and the *aftermath*. That is funnier, and much easier for AI to render.

**Story in one line:** A good boy with a bored nose loses two fights with temptation, then finds something his nose likes even more.

---

## SCENE 1: The Roll (HOOK)
- **Duration:** 0:00–0:03.0
- **Visual:** Bathroom. Goldie's nose is **one centimetre** from the loose end of the toilet paper hanging off the wall roll. He is frozen, and only his eyes slide sideways to the camera. Caught.
- **Camera:** Close-up, side-on at nose height, locked-off. Shallow focus on the nose and paper.
- **Goldie action:** Completely still, nostrils twitching. Slow side-eye to the lens.
- **Product action:** None yet. This is the problem.
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO, quiet, to himself): *"Don't do it, Goldie."*
- **On-screen text:** Captions only.
- **Sound:** Silence and room tone. One tiny tick-tock clock SFX for tension.
- **Transition:** Hard cut, with a paper "rrrip" SFX over the cut.

## SCENE 2: He Did It (FIRST FAIL)
- **Duration:** 0:03.0–0:06.0
- **Visual:** The hallway outside the bathroom is buried in a sea of unrolled toilet paper. Goldie sits in the middle of it, upright and proud-but-guilty, with one strip of paper draped over his head and ear.
- **Camera:** Medium-wide, eye level, static.
- **Goldie action:** Sits still, blinks slowly, ears drop a little (guilty face).
- **Product action:** None.
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO, deflated): *"…Ugh. I'm not supposed to do that."*
- **On-screen text:** Small, top-left: **"attempt #1"**
- **Sound:** Beat of silence, then the line. A soft comedic "womp" is optional, but silence is better.
- **Transition:** Jump cut.

## SCENE 3: The Cushion (SECOND TEMPTATION)
- **Duration:** 0:06.0–0:09.5
- **Visual:** Living room. Goldie stands at the cream sofa with the corner of a throw cushion held gently in his mouth, halfway through pulling it off. He freezes. His eyes slide to the camera again.
- **Camera:** Medium, front 3/4, static.
- **Goldie action:** Mouth gently on the cushion corner, body frozen mid-tug, side-eye.
- **Product action:** None.
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO, defensive): *"This is different. The cushion started it."*
- **On-screen text:** **"attempt #2"**
- **Sound:** Faint fabric-stretch SFX.
- **Transition:** Hard cut on the human's voice.

## SCENE 4: Busted (COMEDY BEAT)
- **Duration:** 0:09.5–0:12.5
- **Visual:** The same sofa. The cushion is on the floor. Goldie sits bolt upright beside it, perfectly still and perfectly innocent, eyes wide, looking at the ceiling.
- **Camera:** Medium, static; a slight snap-zoom in at the start (done in the edit).
- **Goldie action:** Instant "good boy" sit, head high, looking away from the evidence.
- **Product action:** None.
- **Dialogue:**
  - HUMAN (off-screen, sharp): *"GOLDIE."*
  - GOLDIE (VO, innocent): *"Who? Never heard of him."*
- **On-screen text:** Captions (human line in a different colour or italics).
- **Sound:** Record-scratch on "GOLDIE", then silence.
- **Transition:** Cut.

## SCENE 5: The Real Problem (SETUP / PROBLEM)
- **Duration:** 0:12.5–0:15.5
- **Visual:** Goldie lies flat on the jute rug, chin on his paws, eyes wandering: to the toilet-paper roll in the hallway, then back.
- **Camera:** Low, floor-level, static.
- **Goldie action:** Bored sigh (his body deflates), eyes move, ears droop.
- **Product action:** None.
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO, honest): *"Look. I'm bored. And I have a very big nose."*
- **On-screen text:** Captions.
- **Sound:** Big bored exhale SFX, then soft light music starts under it.
- **Transition:** Cut on a sniff: his nose twitches and his head lifts.

## SCENE 6: Wait (DISCOVERY)
- **Duration:** 0:15.5–0:18.5
- **Visual:** Goldie's head pops up and his ears perk. Reverse angle: on the floor nearby lies the **snuffle mat**, with a few treats peeking between the fleece strips.
- **Camera:** Two quick shots joined in the edit: (a) Goldie's head lifting and ears perking, then (b) a low shot of the mat with treats visible.
- **Goldie action:** Head lifts, nostrils work hard, head tilt.
- **Product action:** The mat lies flat and inviting. Treats are visible.
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO, suspicious-curious): *"Wait. Why does the grass smell like chicken?"*
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Sound:** Bright "sniff-sniff" SFX, and the music lifts.
- **Transition:** Match on action: he moves into the mat.

## SCENE 7: Nose Job (DEMO)
- **Duration:** 0:18.5–0:22.0
- **Visual:** Goldie has his nose buried deep in the fleece strips, snuffling, pushing the strips aside, tail wagging hard. He comes up for a moment, chews, then dives back in.
- **Camera:** Close-medium, low side angle, static; the fleece texture is sharp.
- **Goldie action:** Full-focus foraging: nose pushes through strips, head moves side to side, tail wagging.
- **Product action:** Fleece strips part and spring back. Treats get found. **This is the product working.**
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO, muffled, blissed-out): *"Indoor grass. With snacks. Why wasn't I told."*
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Sound:** A layered, satisfying snuffle-snort SFX (the ASMR moment). Music bed continues.
- **Transition:** Cut.

## SCENE 8: So Quiet (PAYOFF)
- **Duration:** 0:22.0–0:25.0
- **Visual:** Wide shot of the living room. Goldie is still happily nose-down in the mat. In the background, the sofa cushions are back in place and nobody is anywhere near the toilet paper.
- **Camera:** Wide, slightly elevated, static.
- **Goldie action:** Keeps foraging. He doesn't even look up.
- **Product action:** The mat is in use, fully visible.
- **Dialogue:**
  - HUMAN (off-screen, suspicious): *"…Goldie? Why is it so quiet?"*
  - GOLDIE (VO, muffled, not looking up): *"Busy."*
- **On-screen text:** Captions.
- **Sound:** Music dips under the human line, then the "Busy." lands dry.
- **Transition:** Slow cross-dissolve.

## SCENE 9: Kept the Couch (CTA)
- **Duration:** 0:25.0–0:28.5
- **Visual:** Goldie lies on his side with his chin resting on the edge of the snuffle mat, content and sleepy, the mat fully readable in frame.
- **Camera:** Medium, front, dog eye-level, very slow push-in.
- **Goldie action:** Slow contented blink, one lazy tail thump.
- **Product action:** The mat is clearly visible (the "this is the product" frame).
- **Dialogue:** GOLDIE (VO, relaxed): *"My indoor grass is in the bio. …The couch says you're welcome."*
- **On-screen text:**
  - Lower third (from 0:25.3): **"Goldie's snuffle mat 🌿 link in bio"**
  - Small, in a bottom corner: **"#ad · Amazon affiliate link · AI-generated video"**
- **Sound:** Music button ending, then a final tiny "sniff" on the last frame.
- **Transition:** Loops back to Scene 1. Nose-to-paper after nose-in-mat is a satisfying loop.
