| Set | "Mango's corner": a natural-wood tabletop play-stand perch by the big living-room window. The pineapple hangs on a short loop from a small stainless-steel hook on the stand's top bar, level with Mango's head. The PetPicks4U living room is blurred behind |
| Status | **Script ready.** Waiting for the owner to approve the product, supply reference photos and pick Mango's voice |

**Core rules:** Mango's beak never lip-syncs, because everything he says is inner voice. Wings stay folded and feet stay on the perch in every shot. There are no treats in shot, and no strands wrapped around him.

## SCENE 1: "Excuse me." (HOOK)
- **SHOT:** SH01
- **DURATION:** 0:00.0–0:03.0 (3.0 s)
- **VISUAL:** A tight two-shot. On the left, Mango is perched in profile. On the right, the colourful woven pineapple hangs at exactly his head height, only a beak-length away. They're almost the same size.
- **MANGO ACTION:** He's looking off to the left, then does the **Snap-Turn**, his head whipping round to face the pineapple, and **freezes**. His feathers lift very slightly.
- **PRODUCT ACTION:** The pineapple hangs still. At most it turns 1–2° on its loop.
- **CAMERA:** Eye level with Mango, static, 85–100 mm macro feel, shallow depth of field, both subjects sharp.
- **DIALOGUE / VO:** MANGO (VO, clipped): *"Excuse me."* (0:00.3) … *"Who invited the pineapple?"* (0:01.3–0:02.8)
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** Burned-in captions of the VO only.
- **SOUND:** Room tone and a single soft "record-stop" tick on the snap-turn. No music yet.
- **TRANSITION:** Hard cut.

## SCENE 2: "Only one fruit" (SETUP)
- **SHOT:** SH02
- **DURATION:** 0:03.0–0:07.0 (4.0 s)
- **VISUAL:** Medium-wide. The whole play-stand perch is visible, with the window light and the blurred living room behind. The pineapple hangs from the hook at the right end of the perch, and Mango is at the left end.
- **MANGO ACTION:** **Sidestep.** He shuffles two or three small steps sideways along the perch toward the pineapple, then stops, chest out.
- **PRODUCT ACTION:** Still. It's clearly readable as a pineapple-shaped toy (the product-ID frame).
- **CAMERA:** Static, slightly below Mango's eye level so he looks bigger than he is.
- **DIALOGUE / VO:**
  - MOM (off-screen, warm, amused): *"It's a toy, Mango. It's for you."* (0:03.2–0:04.9)
  - MANGO (VO, firm): *"There's only one fruit in this house."* (0:05.1–0:06.8)
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** Captions. Mom's line is in italic, in a different colour.
- **SOUND:** A light, bouncy tropical pluck or marimba music bed comes in at about −20 dB on "It's a toy".
- **TRANSITION:** Jump cut in to the close-up.

## SCENE 3: "Under review." (CURIOSITY)
- **SHOT:** SH03
- **DURATION:** 0:07.0–0:09.5 (2.5 s)
- **VISUAL:** Close-up. Mango's head and chest fill the left half of the frame, and the pineapple weave fills the right.
- **MANGO ACTION:** **The Head Tilt.** He turns his head so one eye looks right at the weave, then **leans in** slowly, neck stretched and body still.
- **PRODUCT ACTION:** Still. The palm-leaf texture is sharp.
- **CAMERA:** Static, with a very slow push-in of about 5%.
- **DIALOGUE / VO:** MANGO (VO, lower, suspicious): *"Under review."* (0:07.6–0:08.6)
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** Caption.
- **SOUND:** Music drops to a single held note, plus a faint feather-rustle.
- **TRANSITION:** Cut.

## SCENE 4: The Tug (DEMO)
- **SHOT:** SH04
- **DURATION:** 0:09.5–0:12.5 (3.0 s)
- **VISUAL:** A tight close-up on beak and weave.
- **MANGO ACTION:** **The Tug.** His beak grips the edge of one palm-leaf strip and pulls back with his head. The strip stretches, bends and starts to come loose.
- **PRODUCT ACTION:** The pineapple sways toward him slightly on its loop, then swings back. The weave dents where the strip is pulled.
- **CAMERA:** Static, side angle, and **feet out of frame**.
- **DIALOGUE / VO:** None. The crunch is the moment.
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** None.
- **SOUND:** A **big, crisp dry-leaf CRUNCH / RIP** SFX synced to the pull. The music returns.
- **TRANSITION:** Cut on the action.

## SCENE 5: "Crunch: excellent." (ESCALATION)
- **SHOT:** SH05
- **DURATION:** 0:12.5–0:15.5 (3.0 s), speed-ramped 1.0× to 1.25×
- **VISUAL:** Medium close-up. Mango holds the torn palm strip in his beak beside the pineapple.
- **MANGO ACTION:** **The Flick.** One quick sideways head flick tosses the strip away. It falls out of frame, and he goes straight back toward the pineapple.
- **PRODUCT ACTION:** One strip visibly missing, and a small frayed spot on the weave.
- **CAMERA:** Static.
- **DIALOGUE / VO:** MANGO (VO, satisfied, quick): *"Crunch: excellent."* (0:13.2–0:14.6)
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** Caption.
- **SOUND:** A second smaller crunch, a soft "fwip" on the flick, and a tiny paper-landing tick.
- **TRANSITION:** A hard cut into silence.

## SCENE 6: "It's vintage now." (CONFLICT / PUNCHLINE)
- **SHOT:** SH06
- **DURATION:** 0:15.5–0:20.0 (4.5 s)
- **VISUAL:** Mango faces the camera straight on, **a short palm-leaf strip held in his beak**, with the slightly shredded pineapple beside him.
- **MANGO ACTION:** **The Stare.** He's perfectly still and looking into the lens. On Mom's line, **one slow head tilt**, and he holds it.
- **PRODUCT ACTION:** Still.
- **CAMERA:** Static, eye level, centred.
- **DIALOGUE / VO:**
  - MOM (off-screen, mock-exasperated): *"Mango! That was brand new."* (0:15.7–0:17.0)
  - (a **0.7 s** beat of silence, with the music cut)
  - MANGO (VO, flat, completely unbothered): *"It's vintage now."* (0:17.7–0:18.8)
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** Captions.
- **SOUND:** **Silence** under the beat. The silence is the joke, so no SFX over it.
- **TRANSITION:** Cut.

## SCENE 7: The Floof (PAYOFF / CTA)
- **SHOT:** SH07
- **DURATION:** 0:20.0–0:25.0 (5.0 s)
- **VISUAL:** A medium shot. Mango perched proudly next to the pineapple, which is **still clearly a pineapple** with a few loose strips. Two or three torn strips lie on the table below the perch. Clean negative space in the lower third.
- **MANGO ACTION:** **The Floof.** A whole-body feather puff, a quick shake-out, feathers settle, chest out. On the final beat he turns his head back toward the pineapple, which is the loop trigger.
- **PRODUCT ACTION:** Still and fully visible, the clean "this is the product" frame.
- **CAMERA:** Static, very slow push-in.
- **DIALOGUE / VO:** MANGO (VO, relaxed and smug): *"More pineapples in my bio."* (0:20.6–0:22.0) … *"I'll handle them."* (0:22.6–0:23.6)
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
  - Lower third (0:20.5–end): **"Mango's pineapple 🍍 link in bio"**
  - Small, bottom corner (0:20.5–end): **"#ad · Amazon affiliate link · AI-generated video"**
- **SOUND:** The music resolves with a button ending at 0:24.5, and a soft "fluff" SFX on the floof.
- **TRANSITION:** A hard cut back to the start. His head turning toward the pineapple loops into Scene 1's snap-turn.

## Quality gate (answer before publishing)
1. **Would I stop scrolling?** Watch the first 2 s muted on a phone: bird, pineapple, snap-turn, caption "Excuse me."
2. **Entertainment or ad?** The CTA appears only after 0:20, and Mango never praises features.
3. **Realistic?** Every clip passes `MANGO_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` §10 (colours, 2 wings, correct feet, beak not morphing).
4. **Safe to copy?** Hung short from a stainless hook, no treats, no strands around the bird, and Mom present.
5. **Would a bird owner click?** The product is clearly visible in SH02 and SH07.
