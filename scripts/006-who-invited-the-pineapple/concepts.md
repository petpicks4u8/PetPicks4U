# Video #006 (Mango #1): Five concepts, Planet Pleasures Pineapple (P011)

The goal is to introduce Mango as a character (**small bird, massive confidence**) and show the product doing exactly what it's for, which is getting shredded.
The shared constraints: 15–35 s, 9:16, a hook in the first 1–2 s, one action per clip, wings folded, feet on the perch, silent generation with VO added in the edit.

---

## A. "Who Invited the Pineapple?" (Mango vs. object / rival fruit) ⭐ SELECTED
- **Hook (0–2 s):** Mango's head snaps toward a pineapple hanging right beside him and freezes. VO: *"Excuse me. …Who invited the pineapple?"*
- **Story:** Mom says it's a toy for him. Mango counters: *"There's only one fruit in this house."* He inspects it and tugs a strip. Crunch. Mom: *"Mango! That was brand new."* Mango, strip in beak: *"It's vintage now."*
- **Why it works:** a bird named after a fruit meets a rival fruit, so the joke needs no explanation. It establishes Mango's core trait (everything here is his), shows the real product behaviour (shredding), and ends with a quotable line.
- **AI risk:** medium. There's one hard shot, the tug, and it has a fallback.
- **Series potential:** very high. It kicks off the "rival fruit" gag.

## B. "Open-Concept" (curiosity hook)
- **Hook:** it opens on the aftermath. Mango is perched proudly beside a half-shredded pineapple, with strips on the tray. Mom (off-screen): *"Mango… what happened?"* Mango: *"Renovations."*
- **Story:** "10 minutes earlier", with a quick rewind to the intact pineapple, two shred beats, then back to the present. *"Open-concept. You're welcome."*
- **Why it's good:** strong curiosity, and "Renovation" becomes a recurring format.
- **Why not first:** it opens on a **destroyed** product, so the viewer doesn't see what it is for 3 s. It also needs more debris on screen, which is a higher AI risk. **Ideal as the sequel (video #2 with this product).**

## C. "The Inspection" (Mango POV)
- **Hook:** a close-up of Mango's eye and eye ring in profile. VO: *"Inspection. Begin."*
- **Story:** a formal checklist VO with on-screen ticks: "Colour: acceptable. ✅" "Smell: tropical. ✅" "Crunch: excellent. ✅" "Structural integrity: …" (he tugs a strip) "…not anymore. ❌". Payoff: *"Approved. For demolition."*
- **Why it's good:** very rewatchable, and it creates a repeatable format for every future product ("Mango inspects…").
- **Why not first:** the viewer doesn't know Mango yet, so an inspection joke lands harder once his personality is established. **Planned as Mango video #2 or #3.**

## D. "It's Evidence" (Owner vs. Mango)
- **Hook:** a worn pineapple, with Mom's hand entering the frame holding a brand-new one. Mango, leaning over the old one: *"Don't."*
- **Story:** Mom: *"It's falling apart, bud."* Mango: *"It's evidence."* The swap happens anyway, and Mango inspects the new one: *"…Fine. This one's also mine."*
- **Why it's good:** relatable (owners really do replace these), and it shows the consumable re-buy honestly.
- **Why not first:** a human hand near the bird is an AI risk, and the scene implies an old product and a replacement before we've shown a new one. **Later.**

## E. "Sound On 🔊" (silent ASMR)
- **Hook:** an extreme macro close-up of the beak gripping a palm strip. The crunch is the hook.
- **Story:** no VO. Just 12–15 s of tugs, crunches and one feather-floof, with a single text line: *"he's not destroying it. he's 'foraging.'"*
- **Why it's good:** it's the cheapest version, since it **reuses Concept A's clips**, and it reaches an audience that scrolls muted.
- **Why not first:** it doesn't establish Mango's personality or voice. **Post it 3–5 days after A as a clip-reuse variant**, re-cut with no dialogue so it's a new creative, not a repost.
