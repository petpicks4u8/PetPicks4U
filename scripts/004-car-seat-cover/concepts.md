# Video #004: concepts (URPOWER car seat cover)

Goldie is near-white and extra fluffy, so **fur and mud on Dad's car seats** is the natural joke.

## A. "First Class" ⭐ recommended (~25 s)
1. **Hook (0–3 s):** Dad's back seat is covered in white fur and muddy paw prints. Goldie sits in the middle of it, proud: *"Dad says I'm not allowed in the car anymore."*
2. **Flashback beat (3–7 s):** Goldie does a muddy shake in the back seat, which sends fur and mud everywhere. *"Something about... the fur. And the mud. And the shake."*
3. **Turn (7–10 s):** The door opens and the seat now has the black quilted hammock installed. *"Then Mom and Dad got me THIS."*
4. **Demo (10–20 s):** Goldie hops in with muddy paws and settles on the hammock. Close-ups:
   - "waterproof": water drop beads on the quilting
   - "side flaps cover the doors"
   - "pocket for my toys"
   - Dad wipes a paw print off the cover
   Goldie: *"Waterproof, scratch-resistant, the doors are covered, AND I get a pocket for my toys."*
5. **Payoff (20–23 s):** Goldie lies down like a king, with the window cracked and wind in his fur. *"First class, baby."*
6. **CTA (23–26 s):** *"Dad's seats are safe. It's in my bio!"*

## B. "The Shedding Report"
Goldie reads out his "fur forecast": *"Today: 100% chance of fur."* Then a lint-roller montage of Dad on the seats. The cover goes in, then into the washing machine, and comes out clean. *"Now my fur goes in the washing machine, not in Dad's car."*

## C. "Road Trip Ready"
Goldie "packs" for a trip (toy, ball, snack). He's thrilled at the car, then frozen at Dad's spotless leather seats, *"…I'm not supposed to sit there, am I?"* (callback to his catchphrase). The hammock goes in, Goldie hops in, and the toys go in the pocket. *"Road trip! Woo!"*

## Notes
- Every concept ends with the car parked or Goldie calm on the back seat. No unsafe driving shots.
- The benefits wording comes from the verified listing (waterproof, scratch-resistant, door flaps, pocket, machine washable).
